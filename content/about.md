# About Goal Equity Advisors

Goal Equity Advisors is a research and advisory practice based in Silver Spring, Maryland, founded by Peter Pieh: a youth soccer coach, policy and program management professional, and writer on access and economics in the game. We publish our research under the Football Heritage newsletter.

Our starting point is simple: in American soccer, the scarce resource isn't talent, it's the money to be seen. We make the money, ownership, and tradeoffs in youth soccer legible for DC-area families and the community clubs that serve them.

Goal Equity Advisors publishes research about youth soccer clubs and also sells services to some of them. This page explains how we keep those two things apart, how the research is done, and how to correct it.

## Three commitments

**The research is free and editorial.** Every row on the ownership map and every figure in our reports is built from public filings and primary sources. No club can pay to be added, removed, or rated differently, and declining our services never changes what we publish.

**Verification is paid, and the standard is public.** Open Books verification is a flat-fee review against a published rubric. A client and a non-client face the same criteria, and the fee is due whether the club passes or not.

**Every relationship is disclosed.** Any club that has paid us for anything in the past 24 months is marked as a client wherever it appears in our research.

## How the research is done

### Sources

- **Tax status, revenue, and program spending:** IRS Form 990 filings, retrieved through ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer.
- **Legal entity and status:** Maryland SDAT, the Virginia SCC Clerk's Information System, and DC DLCP CorpOnline.
- **League membership:** league and club announcements and league directories.
- **League ownership:** company press releases, investor disclosures, and reporting that cites them.
- **Facility ownership:** land records, park authority agreements, and facility operator disclosures.
- **Fees and financial aid:** each club's own published pages, as of the research date.

### Confidence

Every claim carries a confidence level. **High** means a primary filing or official source confirms it. **Medium** means a credible secondary source or a single source. **Low** means the claim is inferred and not yet confirmed. Low-confidence claims are shown as unverified, never as fact.

### What we leave out

- **Individual families and players** never appear. Aid figures are published only as club-level totals.
- **Absent firms stay absent.** We do not name an investor as present in a market unless a primary source places it there.

### Keeping it current

Research is re-verified before each fall and spring season. An entry is updated out of cycle, within 10 business days, when any of these happen:

- A league we cover changes owner.
- A club converts from nonprofit to for-profit, merges, or signs a management agreement.
- A club changes leagues or facilities for its top teams.
- A correction is accepted.

Before a new club is added to the ownership map or an existing entry changes materially, we contact the club and invite a response. Responses are published in full alongside the entry.

## Independence and conflicts

- **No money from anyone we rate above the club layer.** Goal Equity Advisors does not accept fees, sponsorship, advertising, or data licensing income from any company that owns or operates a league or facility we cover, or from its investors.
- **Client disclosure.** A club that has paid for any service in the past 24 months carries a client note, naming the service.
- **Work we built, we say so.** Goal Equity Advisors is currently a one-person practice. Until verification is carried out by an evaluator who did not build a club's transparency page, any club whose page we built carries that note on its verification record.
- **Personal affiliations.** Where the founder or a family member coaches at, is registered with, or serves on the board of a club we cover, that entry carries an affiliation note.
- **No quiet removals.** An entry is never removed or softened because a club declined our services, ended an engagement, or objected to its rating. Objections go through the corrections process below, in public.

## Corrections and right of reply

Clubs, league operators, families, and reporters can challenge anything we publish. Send the page, the claim you believe is wrong, and the document that shows it to peterpieh@gmail.com.

We respond within 10 business days. An accepted correction is made to the page and noted with the date and what changed. A rejected challenge receives a written reason, and the club may publish its response alongside the entry.
